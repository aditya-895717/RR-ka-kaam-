Process

How It Works

Every linen item is RFID-tagged and tracked across four scan points — from hospital to laundry and back.

1
Hospital raises order

Hospital staff logs a laundry pickup request with item count and department details.

2
Delivery picks up

Delivery partner scans each RFID-tagged item at the hospital gate (Scan Point S1).

3
Laundry processes

Laundry workers scan items in (S2) and out (S3), updating status in real time.

4
Delivery back to hospital

Items scanned at hospital on return (Scan Point S4). Order marked complete, invoice generated.
